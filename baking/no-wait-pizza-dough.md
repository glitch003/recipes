# No-Wait Pizza Dough

Yield: 1 thin 12-inch pizza (one hungry person)

A baking-powder pizza dough for when you're hungry *now* and don't care about chew, crumb, or yeasty flavor. No rise, no proof, no waiting — mix, roll, top, bake. It's more of a fast flatbread crust than real pizza, and that's the point.

## Ingredients

- 180 g all-purpose flour (1½ cups), plus more for dusting
- 6 g baking powder (1½ tsp)
- 3 g fine sea salt (½ tsp)
- 15 g olive oil (1 Tbsp), plus more for brushing the dough
- 120 g warm water (½ cup), plus a splash more if needed

## Nutrition (estimated)

- **Whole crust (no toppings)**: **835 kcal** — **Fat 18 g**, **Saturated Fat 3 g**, **Carbs 137 g**, **Protein 18 g**, **Fiber 5 g**, **Sugar 0 g**, **Cholesterol 0 mg**

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 550°F (290°C). The high heat crisps the outside before the inside can dry into a biscuit, which is the main failure mode of a baking-powder crust. No need to preheat your perforated pizza pan — the holes let air circulate and steam escape, which crisps the bottom of a thin crust as it bakes.

2. In the KitchenAid bowl, whisk together 180 g flour (1½ cups), 6 g baking powder (1½ tsp), and 3 g salt (½ tsp) by hand to combine.

3. Fit the dough hook. Add 15 g olive oil (1 Tbsp) and 120 g warm water (½ cup), then mix on low (speed 2) for about 1½ to 2 minutes until it forms a soft, slightly tacky ball that clears the sides of the bowl. If it won't come together, add water a teaspoon at a time with the mixer running; if it's sticky, add a spoonful of flour. Don't overwork it — a minute or two is plenty.

4. Dust a countertop with flour and roll or press the dough into a roughly 12-inch round, as thin as you can get it without tearing. Thin is better here — a thick baking-powder crust turns doughy and bland.

5. Brush a little olive oil onto the bottom of the dough round (not onto the pan — oil would just drip through the holes), then lay it on the perforated pizza pan. Poke it all over with a fork so it doesn't balloon.

6. Top lightly — sauce, cheese, whatever's fast. Don't overload it or the crust won't cook through.

7. Bake 7 to 10 minutes, until the edges are browned and the bottom is set. Watch it rather than the clock — at 550°F it moves fast. Eat immediately.
