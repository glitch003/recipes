# Oatmeal Chocolate Chip Cookies (Preppy Kitchen)

Classic chewy oatmeal chocolate chip cookies. Brown sugar keeps them soft and chewy, and chilling the dough deepens the flavor and stops them spreading too thin. Based on [preppykitchen.com](https://preppykitchen.com/oatmeal-chocolate-chip-cookies/).

Makes ~24 cookies.

## Ingredients

- 120 g all-purpose flour (1 cup)
- 3 g baking soda (½ tsp)
- 5 g salt (½ tsp)
- 113 g unsalted butter, room temperature (½ cup)
- 65 g granulated sugar (⅓ cup)
- 140 g light brown sugar, packed (⅔ cup)
- 10 mL pure vanilla extract (2 tsp)
- 1 large egg
- 135 g rolled oats (1½ cups)
- 150 g semisweet chocolate chips (1 cup)
- 80 g raisins (½ cup)

## Notes

- **Chill the dough** for at least 1 hour (up to overnight) — this is the key step for thick, chewy cookies that don't spread flat.
- Cookies are done when the edges start to brown but the centers are still soft — they'll finish setting on the pan.
- Optional add-ins: ½–1 cup toasted chopped walnuts, or swap some chips for chocolate chunks for a more rustic look.
- Measure flour by fluffing, spooning into the cup, and leveling — or just use a scale.

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together **120 g flour** and **3 g baking soda**. Set aside.

2. In a large bowl, beat **113 g room-temperature butter** with **65 g granulated sugar** and **140 g brown sugar** on medium speed until light and fluffy.

3. Reduce speed to low and add **5 g salt**, **10 mL vanilla**, and **1 egg**. Beat until well mixed, about 1 minute.

4. Add the flour mixture and mix until almost combined.

5. Stir in **135 g rolled oats**, **150 g chocolate chips**, and **80 g raisins**.

6. Chill the dough 1 hour to overnight.

7. Preheat oven to 375 °F (190 °C). Line baking sheets with parchment.

8. Drop heaping ~2-tablespoon balls about 2 inches apart on the sheets.

9. Bake 8–10 minutes, until golden around the edges but still soft in the center.

10. Cool on the baking sheet 1–2 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.
