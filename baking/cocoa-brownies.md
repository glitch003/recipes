# Cocoa Brownies

Yield: ~16 brownies (8-inch square pan)

Inspired by Alice Medrich's Cocoa Brownies, which used cocoa powder instead of melted chocolate for better ingredient control — moist, fudgy center with a shiny, candy-like top.

## Ingredients

- 142 g unsalted butter (10 Tbsp)
- 250 g granulated sugar (1¼ cups)
- 75 g unsweetened cocoa powder, spooned and leveled (¾ cup plus 2 Tbsp)
- 5 g vanilla extract (1 tsp)
- 1.5 g fine sea salt (¼ tsp)
- 2 large cold eggs
- 65 g all-purpose flour, spooned and leveled (½ cup)
- 80 g chopped walnuts, pecans, or chocolate chips (⅔ cup, optional)

## Nutrition (estimated)

- **Per brownie (1 of 16)**: **158 kcal** — **Fat 8 g**, **Saturated Fat 5 g**, **Carbs 21 g**, **Protein 2 g**, **Fiber 2 g**, **Sugar 16 g**, **Cholesterol 42 mg**

## Instructions

1. Position an oven rack in the middle and heat to 325°F (162°C). Line the bottom and sides of an 8-inch square baking pan with parchment paper or aluminum foil, leaving an overhang on opposite sides to lift the baked brownies out.

2. Add 142 g butter (10 Tbsp) to a medium saucepan over medium-low heat and cook until it melts completely, then turn off the heat. Stay close and do not let it brown.

3. While the butter is hot, stir in 250 g sugar (1¼ cups), 75 g cocoa powder (¾ cup plus 2 Tbsp), 5 g vanilla (1 tsp), and 1.5 g fine sea salt (¼ tsp) until well blended. Don't worry if the batter looks gritty — it smooths out once the eggs go in.

4. Set the saucepan aside to cool until the mixture is warm, not hot, 5 to 10 minutes. It should still be quite warm when you touch it, but not so hot you need to pull away.

5. Add 2 large cold eggs one at a time, stirring vigorously after each.

6. When the batter looks thick and well blended, add 65 g flour (½ cup). Beat it in with a wooden spoon or spatula until very thick and it pulls away from the sides — about 40 to 50 strokes.

7. If using, stir in 80 g chopped nuts or chocolate chips (⅔ cup).

8. Scrape the thick batter into the prepared pan and spread evenly, pushing into the corners. Bake 20 to 30 minutes, until the edges look set and the middle is slightly underbaked. A toothpick in the center should come out with somewhat moist batter.

9. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan so they set properly. Cut with a sharp chef's knife, wiping the blade clean after each slice for clean edges. For even cleaner edges, chill 1 to 2 hours before slicing.

## Storage

Airtight container at room temp for 2 to 3 days, or in the fridge for 2 weeks. Freeze up to 3 months — wrap individually to pull out one or two at a time.

## Notes

- **Baking pan**: Metal 8-inch square is ideal. Ceramic and glass may affect bake time — check 5 minutes early, then every 5 minutes. Double the recipe for a 9×13-inch pan (brownies will be slightly thicker).
- **Cocoa powder**: Dutch-process gives a mellow, Oreo-like flavor; natural cocoa (Ghirardelli, Guittard) gives a rich, dark chocolate taste.
- **Measuring flour and cocoa**: Weigh them, or use the "spoon and level" method — fluff, spoon into the cup, level off the top.
- **Doneness**: Brownies continue to cook and firm up as they cool, so don't be afraid to pull them with the center still a little underdone.
- **Double-boiler method**: The original recipe used a double boiler. To use it, place a heat-safe bowl with the butter, sugar, and cocoa powder over barely simmering water. Once melted, cool and continue as directed.
