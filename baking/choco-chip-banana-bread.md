# Chocolate Chip Banana Bread

Yield: 1 loaf (~10 slices, 8×4-inch pan)

Classic one-bowl banana bread, with no mixer needed. It works with 2 bananas. Based on Elise Bauer's [Simply Recipes banana bread](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/banana_bread/) (originally Mrs. Hockmeyer's), with chocolate chips added.

## Ingredients

- 2 very ripe bananas, peeled (~230 g)
- 76 g salted butter, melted (⅓ cup)
- 5.8 g baking soda (1 tsp)
- ~1 g kosher salt (a pinch; skip if using salted butter)
- 150 g granulated sugar (¾ cup; use 100 g for less sweet, 200 g for sweeter)
- 1 large egg, beaten (~50 g without shell)
- 5 g vanilla extract (1 tsp)
- 180 g all-purpose flour, spooned and leveled (1½ cups)
- 120 g semisweet chocolate chips (⅔ cup), plus a small handful for the top

## Nutrition (estimated)

- **Per slice (1 of 10)**: **270 kcal** — **Fat 9.9 g**, **Carbs 41.5 g**, **Protein 3.3 g**, **Fiber 1.8 g**, **Sugar 23.7 g**, **Sodium ~183 mg**
- **Per loaf**: **2,700 kcal** — **Fat 99 g**, **Carbs 415 g**, **Protein 32.5 g**, **Fiber 18 g**, **Sugar 237 g**, **Sodium ~1,830 mg**
- **Assumptions**: salted butter, no added salt; semisweet chips modeled at ~500 kcal / 100 g; 2 medium bananas (~230 g); topping chips not counted.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Butter an 8×4-inch loaf pan.

2. In a medium bowl, mash **2 bananas** with a fork until mostly smooth. Stir in **76 g melted salted butter**.

3. Mix in **5.8 g baking soda** (plus **a pinch of salt** only if using unsalted butter). Stir in **150 g sugar**, **1 beaten egg**, and **5 g vanilla**.

4. Toss **120 g chocolate chips** with a spoonful of the **180 g flour** (this keeps them from sinking). Add the rest of the flour and fold until just combined, then fold in the chips. Don't overmix.

5. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and scatter a few extra chips on top.

6. Bake 50–60 minutes, until a toothpick or knife in the center comes out clean. A melted chip can look like wet batter, so test in a couple of spots. If the top browns too fast, tent it loosely with foil for the last 15 minutes.

7. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then lift out onto a wire rack and cool before slicing.

## Storage

Wrap tightly and keep at room temp for 2–3 days, or in the fridge for up to a week. Freeze slices up to 3 months.

## Notes

- **Bananas**: The browner the better. Heavily speckled or black bananas are sweeter and have more banana flavor. With only 2 bananas the loaf is a little less dense than with 3, which is fine.
- **Pan size**: A 9×5-inch pan works too. The loaf will be flatter, so start checking at 45 minutes.
- **Variations**: Swap half the flour for whole wheat, or add ½ cup chopped toasted walnuts.
