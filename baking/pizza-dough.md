# Master Lean Dough (Pizza / Rolls / Breadsticks / Focaccia)

One dough, many uses. Makes ~900g — three to four thin 12" pizzas (or two thicker/larger pies), ~12 dinner rolls, ~16 breadsticks, or one half-sheet focaccia.

## Base Recipe

- 500g bread flour (AP works; bread flour gives better chew)
- 325g water, room temp (65% hydration)
- 10g salt — either granulated/fine sea salt or kosher works (granulated preferred since it dissolves more evenly; if using kosher, weigh it rather than measuring by volume)
- 3g instant yeast (~1 tsp) for same-day, OR 1g (~¼ tsp) for overnight cold ferment
- 10g olive oil (optional — softens crumb; skip for crispier pizza)

## Method

1. Mix 500g flour, 3g (or 1g) yeast, and 325g water until no dry bits. Rest 20 min (autolyse).
2. Add 10g salt + 10g oil. Knead 8–10 min by hand, or 5 min in a stand mixer on low, until smooth and passes the windowpane test.
3. **Bulk ferment:** 1.5–2 hrs at room temp until doubled, OR (better flavor) 24–72 hrs in the fridge.
4. Divide and shape. Bench rest 30–60 min before baking so the gluten relaxes.

The overnight cold ferment is the single biggest flavor upgrade — worth planning ahead.

## Per-Use Adjustments

### Pizza
- 250g balls, stretch thin
- Bake on steel or stone at the highest temp your oven hits (550°F+), 6–8 min
- For ovens that go above 600°F, bump hydration to 70% (350g water)

### Dinner Rolls
- 70g balls, tucked into tight rounds, placed in a buttered 9×13
- Proof 45 min covered
- Brush with melted butter, bake 375°F for 20–22 min until golden
- Brush with more butter out of the oven

### Breadsticks
- 60g pieces rolled into 8" ropes
- Place on parchment, proof 30 min
- Brush with olive oil, sprinkle flaky salt + parmesan + garlic powder
- Bake 425°F for 12–15 min

### Garlic Knots
- 50–75g pieces rolled into 6–8" ropes, tied in a single overhand knot, tuck the tails under
- Place on parchment with ~1" between them, proof 30 min covered
- Optional egg wash for shine, or just brush with olive oil
- Bake 425°F for 12–14 min until golden on top and bottom

**Garlic butter (for 4 knots):**
- 3 tbsp butter, melted
- 2 cloves garlic, finely minced (or grated on a microplane)
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley
- 2 tbsp grated parmesan
- pinch of flaky salt
- optional: pinch of red pepper flakes

Warm the butter with the garlic over low heat for ~1 min (don't brown it — you want it infused, not toasted). Off heat, stir in parsley. Toss the hot knots in the butter in a bowl, then sprinkle with parmesan and salt. Serve immediately.

### Focaccia
- Press whole batch into an oiled half-sheet pan
- Dimple with oiled fingers, top with olive oil + flaky salt + rosemary
- Proof 45 min, bake 450°F for 20–25 min

---

## Notes

- For rolls/breadsticks, dropping hydration to 60% (300g water) makes shaping easier.
- Salt is 2% of flour weight, yeast is 0.6% (same-day) or 0.2% (cold ferment) — scale the recipe by adjusting flour and keeping these ratios.
