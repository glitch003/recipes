# Oatmeal Chocolate Chip Cookies (Cakey, with Greek Yogurt)

Greek yogurt swaps in for the whey and pushes these toward a softer, more bread-y/cakey texture. The yogurt brings moisture, a little tang, and protein. Baking powder joins the baking soda to lift them. Skip the lemon/vinegar — the yogurt is already acidic enough to wake up the baking soda.

## Ingredients

- 140 g unsalted butter, softened (dropped further — more yogurt means more moisture and a little extra fat)
- 300 g white sugar
- 8 g pure vanilla extract
- 3 large eggs (~150 g without shells)
- 300 g plain Greek yogurt (full-fat or 2%, ~30 g protein)
- 370 g bread flour (bumped to soak up the extra yogurt)
- 220 g rolled oats (old-fashioned, not quick)
- 7 g baking soda
- 9 g kosher salt
- 340 g chocolate chips

## Notes

- **Cakier than chewy.** The yogurt + baking powder combo makes them puff and stay soft. They won't spread much.
- Use **full-fat** yogurt if you have it — nonfat works but the cookies get a little drier on day 2.
- These keep their shape, so flatten the dough balls a bit before baking if you want them less domed.
- They go stale faster than the all-butter version (more water in the dough). Store airtight.
- **Bread flour** adds a few extra grams of protein and leans into the bread-y character — just don't overmix once it's in, or they'll turn tough.

## Nutrition (estimated)

- **Per ~35 g cookie**: **120 kcal** — **Fat 4.7 g**, **Carbs 17.5 g**, **Protein 3.0 g**, **Fiber 1.0 g**, **Sugar 10.5 g**
- **Per batch (~1.85 kg dough)**: **6,350 kcal** — **Fat 250 g**, **Carbs 925 g**, **Protein 160 g**, **Fiber 51 g**, **Sugar 555 g**
- **Assumptions**: ~35 g cookie size; semisweet chips at ~500 kcal/100 g; 2% Greek yogurt.

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 °F (175 °C) — slightly lower than the chewy version so the centers can set without over-browning the edges. Line baking sheets with parchment.

2. In a medium bowl, whisk together **370 g bread flour**, **220 g rolled oats**, **7 g baking soda**, and **9 g kosher salt**. Set aside.

3. In a large bowl, cream **140 g softened unsalted butter** with **300 g white sugar** until combined and fluffy.

4. Beat in **3 large eggs** and **8 g vanilla extract** until light (about 1 minute). Then beat in **300 g plain Greek yogurt** until smooth — it'll look a little curdled, that's fine.

5. Mix in the dry ingredients until just combined.

6. Fold in **340 g chocolate chips**. The dough will be softer/wetter than a typical cookie dough.

7. Scoop 30-45 g portions onto the sheets. Flatten slightly with your palm if you want them less domed — they won't spread much on their own.

8. Bake 11-13 minutes. Pull them when the tops look set and the edges are just barely golden.

9. Rest on the pan 5 minutes, then move to a cooling rack.
