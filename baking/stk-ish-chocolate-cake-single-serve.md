# Eggless STK-ish Chocolate Cake for One Ramekin

A single-serve, eggless, toaster-oven "STK-ish" version of the STK Steakhouse chocolate cake: dark cake + ganache/fudge + optional crunch.

STK's menu describes its chocolate cake with chocolate ganache sauce, and a pastry partner post describes a more elaborate version with chocolate cake, dark chocolate mousse, chocolate buttercream, feuilletine crunch, and a hot fudge pour-over.

Yield: 1 ramekin (use an oven-safe 6 oz ramekin if you have one — the little white mise-en-place dish)

## Ingredients

### Cake

- 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 1/2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/8 tsp baking soda
- tiny pinch baking powder (optional)
- pinch salt
- 3 tbsp milk (any kind)
- 1 tbsp neutral oil or melted butter
- 1/4 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 tsp vinegar or lemon juice
- 1 tbsp hot coffee or hot water

### Fast ganache / hot fudge pour-over

- 2 tbsp chocolate chips or chopped chocolate
- 1 tbsp cream, milk, or half-and-half
- 1/2 tsp butter (optional but good)
- pinch salt

### Optional STK-ish crunch

- 1 tbsp crushed cornflakes, wafer cookie, Rice Krispies, or pretzel
- 1 tsp melted chocolate or Nutella
- tiny pinch salt

## Instructions

1. **Cake:** In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda, baking powder, and salt. Add the milk, oil, vanilla, vinegar, and hot coffee. Stir until smooth.

2. Grease the ramekin and pour in the batter. Bake in a toaster oven at **325°F on bake mode** for **16–22 minutes**, until the top springs back and a toothpick has a few moist crumbs. Don't overbake — STK's cake is decadent but balanced by airy cake layers.

3. **Ganache:** Microwave or gently heat the chocolate, cream, butter, and salt. Stir until glossy. Add a splash more milk if you want it pourable.

4. **Crunch (optional):** Mix the crushed cereal/cookie with melted chocolate or Nutella and a pinch of salt. Put it on top before the ganache, or layer it in the middle to be fancy.

5. **Assembly:** Let the cake cool 5 minutes. Poke a few holes in the top, pour over warm ganache, add the crunch, then hit it with a final drizzle of ganache. For extra steakhouse-dessert energy, add whipped cream or vanilla ice cream.

## Notes — non-alkalized (natural) cocoa

I'm using non-alkalized/natural cocoa right now, and it actually works well here because the recipe uses baking soda, which likes acidity. Natural cocoa makes the cake a little more sharp, fruity, and old-school chocolatey, but slightly less dark/"Oreo-black" than Dutch-process cocoa.

Small tweaks for the natural-cocoa version:

- Keep 1/8 tsp baking soda
- Use only **1/4 tsp** vinegar or lemon juice instead of 1/2 tsp
- Keep the 1 tbsp hot coffee or hot water
- Add a slightly generous pinch of salt

**Best move: bloom the cocoa first.** Mix the cocoa powder with the hot coffee/water into a paste before adding the rest of the wet ingredients. This deepens the flavor and avoids the "health food cocoa powder" taste.

So the wet part becomes:

- 3 tbsp milk
- 1 tbsp oil or melted butter
- 1/4 tsp vanilla
- 1/4 tsp vinegar/lemon juice
- 1 tbsp hot coffee/water mixed first with the cocoa

For a more STK-ish darker flavor, let the ganache do the heavy lifting: use chocolate chips/chopped dark chocolate, a pinch of salt, and maybe a tiny splash of coffee in the ganache too.
