# Ground Beef Sloppy Joes

Yield: 4 sandwiches

Adapted from a chickpea-and-lentil sloppy joe recipe (from a diet-tracking app), swapped back to ground beef. The sauce is the same idea as the original — tomato sauce, mustard, honey, garlic powder, and a little cayenne — but the beef renders its own fat and gives up water as it cooks, so the olive oil and the added water from the original are cut way down. Amounts for the onion, pepper, and sauce were inferred from the original's method and from standard 1-lb sloppy joe recipes; the original only listed the beans, lentils, buns, and lettuce.

## Ingredients

- 454 g lean ground beef (1 lb, 90/10)
- 5 g olive oil (1 tsp) — optional, only if your pan isn't nonstick
- 150 g yellow onion, finely diced (1 small)
- 150 g green or red bell pepper, finely diced (1 medium)
- 245 g tomato sauce (1 cup / one 8-oz can)
- 15 g Dijon mustard (1 Tbsp)
- 21 g honey (1 Tbsp)
- 3 g garlic powder (1 tsp)
- 3 g salt (½ tsp), less if your tomato sauce is salted
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (more if you like it spicy)
- 15–45 g water (1–3 Tbsp), as needed to loosen the sauce
- 4 whole-wheat hamburger buns, toasted
- 4 leaves romaine lettuce

Optional, not in the original but standard in beef sloppy joes: 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce and 1 Tbsp ketchup, added with the tomato sauce.

## Nutrition (estimated)

- **Per full recipe (4 sandwiches)**:  
  **~1,600 kcal** — **Fat 58 g**, **Carbs 150 g**, **Protein 120 g**, **Fiber 22 g**, **Sugar 49 g**, **Sodium ~3,200 mg**

- **Per sandwich (¼ recipe, with bun)**:  
  **~400 kcal** — **Fat 14.5 g**, **Carbs 38 g**, **Protein 30 g**, **Fiber 5.5 g**, **Sugar 12 g**, **Sodium ~800 mg**

- **Filling only, per serving (no bun)**:  
  **~270 kcal** — **Fat 12.5 g**, **Carbs 14 g**, **Protein 24 g**

- **Assumptions**:
  - Beef modeled as raw 90/10 ground beef, rendered fat left in the pan
  - Tomato sauce modeled as regular (salted) canned tomato sauce; low-sodium cuts sodium by ~250 mg per serving
  - Buns modeled as ~50 g whole-wheat hamburger buns (130 kcal each)
  - Optional Worcestershire and ketchup not included
  - Nutrition is an estimate and will vary by brand

## Instructions

1. Heat a large sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add the ground beef (and the teaspoon of oil if the pan isn't nonstick) and break it up with a spatula. Cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and no pink remains, about 5 to 7 minutes. If there's more than a tablespoon or so of fat in the pan, tilt and spoon off the excess.

2. Reduce heat to medium. Add the onion and bell pepper to the beef and sauté, stirring occasionally, until tender, about 4 to 5 minutes.

3. Add the tomato sauce, mustard, honey, garlic powder, salt, cayenne, and 1 Tbsp of water (plus Worcestershire and ketchup if using). Stir to combine and scrape up anything stuck to the pan.

4. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 10 minutes so the flavors meld and the sauce thickens into the beef. Check once partway through — if it looks dry or is catching on the bottom, stir in another 1 to 2 Tbsp of water. If it's too loose, uncover for the last few minutes.

5. Taste and adjust: more salt, more cayenne, or a little more honey if the tomato is sharp.

6. Toast the buns. Pile the beef onto the bottom buns, top each with a romaine leaf and the top bun, and serve immediately.

## Notes

- **Lean vs. regular beef**: 80/20 works and tastes richer, but drain most of the fat after browning or the sauce gets greasy. Adds roughly 60 kcal per sandwich.
- **Make-ahead**: the filling keeps 3 to 4 days in the fridge and reheats well with a splash of water.
