# Momofuku Green Bean Casserole

Yield: about 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 2 pounds green beans
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 3 tablespoons white miso
- 1 tablespoon Momofuku Garlic Crunch
- 1 cup store-bought crispy onions, for topping
- Sliced scallions, for serving

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and add the kosher salt. Prepare a large bowl of ice water. Boil the green beans for 6 minutes, reserving 1 cup of the hot cooking water before draining. Immediately plunge the beans into the ice water.
3. In a large ovenproof skillet or braiser, melt the butter over medium heat. Stir in the flour and keep stirring for 3 minutes.
4. Whisk the reserved cup of bean water into the flour mixture, then pour in the cream. Whisk in the miso and Garlic Crunch until smooth. Simmer for 3–4 minutes, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens.
5. Drain the beans and toss them in the sauce. Scatter the crispy onions over the beans. Bake for 25 minutes at 375°F, until the sauce bubbles and the beans are tender.
6. Sprinkle with sliced scallions just before serving.
