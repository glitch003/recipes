# Roasted Butternut Squash Lasagna

Serves 8–10.

## Ingredients

- Butternut squash, 3 lb, peeled, seeded and cut into ½-inch cubes
- Olive or grapeseed oil, 2 Tbsp for roasting
- Whole or 2% milk, 4 cups
- Fresh sage and rosemary, a few sprigs
- Unsalted butter, 4 Tbsp, plus extra for the dish
- Minced garlic, 1 Tbsp
- All-purpose flour, ¼ cup
- 9 no-boil lasagna sheets (7 × 3½ inches)
- Grated Parmesan, 1⅓ cups
- Heavy cream, 1 cup
- Salt, ½ tsp for the cream, plus salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Toss squash with oil and salt. Roast in a single layer at 450°F for 30 minutes, turning halfway, until tender and golden.
2. Meanwhile, simmer milk with herbs for 10 minutes, then strain.
3. Soften garlic in butter over medium-low heat. Stir in flour for 3 minutes. Off heat, gradually whisk in milk. Simmer for 10 minutes until thickened. Fold in squash and season.
4. Butter a 9 × 13-inch dish. Lower oven to 375°F. Soak sheets in hot water for 2–3 minutes, three at a time as you assemble.
5. Start with 1 cup sauce. Add three sheets, half the remaining sauce and ½ cup Parmesan. Repeat with three sheets, the remaining sauce and another ½ cup Parmesan. Finish with three sheets.
6. Whip cream with ½ tsp salt to soft peaks. Cover pasta completely with cream and scatter remaining Parmesan over it. Tent tightly with foil. Bake 30 minutes covered, then 10–20 minutes uncovered until golden and bubbling. Rest 5 minutes before slicing.
