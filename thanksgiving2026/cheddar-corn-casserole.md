# Cheddar Corn Casserole

Allow 10 minutes for prep, 50–55 minutes for baking, and at least 5 minutes to rest.

## Ingredients

- 1 can (15¼ oz) whole kernel corn, drained
- 1 can (14¾ oz) cream-style corn
- 1 package (8 oz) corn muffin mix (Jiffy recommended)
- 1 cup sour cream
- ½ stick (4 Tbsp) butter, melted
- 1–1½ cups shredded Cheddar cheese

## Directions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9 × 13-inch casserole dish.
2. In a large bowl, stir together both cans of corn, the corn muffin mix, sour cream, and melted butter. Pour the mixture into the prepared dish.
3. Bake for about 45 minutes, until golden brown.
4. Sprinkle the Cheddar evenly over the casserole. Return it to the oven for 5–10 minutes, until the cheese melts.
5. Let the casserole stand for at least 5 minutes. Serve warm.
