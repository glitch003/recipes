# French Onion Scalloped Potatoes

Yield: 8–10 servings (one 4-quart casserole)

## Ingredients

### Caramelized onions

- 42 g unsalted butter (3 Tbsp)
- 10–12 medium sweet onions, thinly sliced
- 1 Tbsp Morton kosher salt or 2 Tbsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt

### Scalloped potatoes

- 85 g unsalted butter (6 Tbsp), at room temperature, divided
- 2.27–2.72 kg medium or large Yukon Gold potatoes (5–6 lb), sliced ⅛–¼ inch thick, using a mandoline if desired
- 475 g heavy cream (2 cups)
- 1 Tbsp Morton kosher salt or 2 Tbsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 7 g freshly ground black pepper (1 Tbsp), plus more for garnish
- 680 g Gruyère (1½ lb), rind removed, coarsely grated with a box grater or food processor
- 6 g fresh chives (2 Tbsp), minced

## Nutrition (estimated)

- **Full recipe** (~2.5 kg potatoes, ~1.2 kg trimmed onions, full-fat dairy): ~7,700 kcal, 500 g fat, 550 g carbs, 280 g protein.
- **Per serving** (⅒ recipe): ~770 kcal, 50 g fat, 55 g carbs, 28 g protein.
- **Per serving** (⅛ recipe): ~960 kcal, 63 g fat, 69 g carbs, 35 g protein.

## Instructions

1. Melt 42 g (3 Tbsp) butter in a large Dutch oven or pot over medium heat. Stir in the onions and their portion of salt. Cover, turn the heat to high, and cook for 15 minutes to reduce the volume of the onions.
2. Uncover and reduce the heat to medium-low. Cook for about 1½ hours, stirring occasionally and scraping the bottom, until the onions are deep golden brown and the liquid has evaporated.
3. Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Grease a 4-quart casserole dish with 28 g (2 Tbsp) of the room-temperature butter.
4. Combine the cream, remaining 57 g (4 Tbsp) butter, potato portion of salt, and black pepper in a small saucepan. Bring to a bare simmer over medium-low heat to melt the butter.
5. Arrange one-third of the potatoes in a flat layer in the dish. Top with one-third of the onions and one-third of the Gruyère. Repeat once. For the third layer, overlap the potato slices in a spiral, then top with the remaining onions and cheese. The dish will be full.
6. Drizzle the cream mixture over the potatoes, pouring some around the edges so it reaches the bottom. Grease a sheet of parchment and lay it greased side down over the casserole. Cover tightly with foil and set the dish on a foil-lined baking sheet.
7. Bake for 1 hour. Remove the foil and parchment, raise the oven to 450°F (230°C), and bake for another 40–45 minutes, until the cream bubbles, the cheese is deep golden, and a paring knife slips easily through the potatoes. Garnish with the chives and more black pepper. Serve hot or warm.

## Notes

- The caramelized onions can be made ahead. Refrigerate in a sealed container for up to 5 days or freeze for up to a few months.
- For a half batch, halve all the ingredients and use a 2-quart casserole dish.
